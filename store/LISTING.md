# Chrome Web Store listing draft

Prepared October 20, 2026. Do not describe this draft as an approved store release.

## Name

Phase Pal Music Bridge

## Short description

Connect browser audio to Phase Pal Dance to Music, with optional local beat sync and custom websites.

## Detailed description

Make your Phase Pal desktop companion react to browser playback, with optional
beat and tempo analysis performed on your device.

REQUIRES THE PHASE PAL DESKTOP APP
A compatible, updated Phase Pal desktop app with its local browser bridge is
required. Enable Dance to Music in the app. This extension is a companion feature;
it does not play music or work as a standalone avatar. Supported app builds target
Windows and macOS. Linux is not supported by this release workflow.

WHAT IT DOES
- Reports playing/paused state from built-in music websites to the local app.
- Offers Enable Beat Sync for the active tab, then Stop Beat Sync to end analysis.
- Calculates energy, estimated tempo, beat confidence and timing locally.
- Lets you add an exact custom website with Chrome's permission and remove it later.

MUSIC SERVICES
Built-in integrations include Spotify Web, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, Apple Music,
Amazon Music regional players, Deezer, TIDAL, Qobuz, Pandora, Yandex Music,
QQ Music, KuGou, JioSaavn, LINE MUSIC, Bugs, JOOX, Anghami, and Audiomack.
Availability depends on your account, subscription, region, and the site's player.
Custom sites are optional and use separate per-site access requests.

YOUR AUDIO AND DATA
Beat Sync captures only the tab you choose after you click the popup control.
Audio remains on your device and the tab stays audible. The bridge sends fixed
playback flags and rhythm values to the local Phase Pal app. It does not send song
names, artist names, URLs, page text, browser history, recordings, or raw audio to
the app. It does not request microphone access, transcribe speech, or upload audio.
Custom website origins are saved locally in Chrome; paths and query strings are
not saved. The extension has no advertising, analytics, or remote upload endpoint.

LIMITS
Supported-site playback is treated as music: podcasts, speech, videos, previews,
and ads may also trigger dancing. Beat estimates are not guaranteed. Protected
content, restricted pages, login requirements, subscriptions, and regional limits
still apply. The extension does not bypass them.
