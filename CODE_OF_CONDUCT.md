# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss the work and give clear,
constructive feedback. Accept good-faith questions and differing views.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, spam, and sharing another
person's private information are not allowed in project spaces.

Maintainers may remove content, close contributions, or block participation when
needed to protect the community. Responses should reflect the severity and
pattern of the conduct. These rules also apply to maintainers.

For a private conduct concern, email
[hello@consistencyai.net](mailto:hello@consistencyai.net). Do not repeat private
information in public reports. GitHub's reporting tools are also available for
abuse on GitHub.
